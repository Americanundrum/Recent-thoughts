i was on ponytown actually, and i was playing with my other friends. now keep in mind this is only a portion of the dream, the rest of it was unrelated. but i was on ponytown, it was snowy, and i was sitting in the CH area in my usual zone. i was playing with some other friends and i dont think i recognized their skin actually, i couldnt recognize them, i think they were friends the dream made up. but we were chasing each other around and they led me to this area past a stone wall, and there you were. you were in a skin ive seen you in before. it was a simple one. and you were sitting with them. i felt such mixed feelings, but all i felt was happiness to see you again. it was such a silly thing to even dream about because we've only talked once, but i guess i've been wondering where you were. but you said, "sorry. [◉] told me i had to sit somewhere else, that i couldnt sit there around you anymore." and i think i felt such a deep feeling of dread in me, because then it would mean that all my fears were true. that you had disappeared because they didnt want you around me. i was sick. 

i asked you "what? why not? you were really nice to talk to, i missed you. what did he say about me?" but you ignored me and went on to ask me how my day was. i told you that it was fine. i told you that im just glad i got to see you again, but then you never responded. and i guess i walked back after my friends ushered me to join them. and i never saw you again after that. i never even got to ask what to call you. you were one of the few to have cared about me, and i thought i actually had a chance. but now it feels like the world is trying to tear me apart from all these old things and it's just confirming my beliefs that the universe is trying to stop me from grieving. 

i wish i stayed up later that night. i wish i took pictures of how we talked. it was once, but i think we wouldve gotten along. and i still dont know what to call you, so thats why im writing this entry in such a way. if you came back just this once and told me your name.

... 

i think i'm overstepping my place. im mentally ill. i know. im very mentally ill. i continued dreaming after that. i was no longer on ponytown, but me and my group made a trek somewhere through the darkness of the snow and we entered this tropical themed area with tikis and huts and we entered a hut and went down this tunnel, away from the snow. we walked, we walked, and began hiking through this long.. long, trail. a trail of tall, golden grass that twisted and flicked, and beyond that grass were wild thundering tornadoes, whipping blue lightning through the clouds. i didnt think about you when i walked. i wish i did. i wish i kept dreaming about our conversations.

i want to keep dreaming about these conversations. theyre the only way i can feel close to the people ive lost. it's so humiliating, it really is, but it's the only way. because in reality, maybe you moved servers or maybe you blocked me. im sorry if i ever said anything to set you away. but i'll always remember that night i caught a glimpse of hope. now i keep looking in that area hoping that you're there like you usually are, but you aren't. and i think i've made a grave mistake. if we do meet again, i'd like to apologize for anything i've done wrong. i told myself that in my next friendships, i'd start taking steps to be a better friend, but it seems i've already miscalculated. i wish i could try again. i wish i could prove my worth. i wish i  knew what i did wrong. i wish people would tell me why they left. come on. im human to you, right?
