 serenity
<p align="center">
<img width="500" src=https://imgur.com/6VFtyin.png>

i have a genuine belief that the universe, fate, some kind of force is trying to make my life better for me. it's given me the easy way and yet i'm still complaining. why? well, it has given me new chances to branch outside of what i once had. people have come up to me complimenting me, asking if we could be friends, telling me i look cool. i appreciate it, but i feel nothing deep inside. i'm glad, i truly am, it's just that it doesn't move me as much as i wish it did. there are some experiences where i go -- "oh! they would love to hear about this" but then i stop and realize that i'll never be able to really replicate that feeling of being able to share something stupid and weird with someone and knowing they'll laugh at that specific joke with me because of what it carries. 

i've made new friends. they're all nice. it doesn't feel the same. i remember talking to Redcoat and i asked him if he would ever leave me, and that if he ever did then that he would tell me why and explain what i can do to be better. i told them that i was willing to act however they wanted me to if it made them happy, if i could ever replicate what their old friend was like to make them happier. but they told me that they would never make me do a thing like that. and i guess for a second, i questioned my worth, as if i wasnt able to act for someone then i wasnt able to be of any use. I want to prove to him that I can be useful, that i can fulfill my end of this.. platonic transaction. why should one party have to put in their all when the other sits there and takes it for granted?

i've had nights where i was laughing and giggling the same as i was when we would still talk. i was laughing a bunch. one of my new friends, Makarov, he's great. he and i share similar interests and he's really good at drawing, as am i. we talked about our soldiers and i showed him my oc, and he told me that he really liked him. then he showed me his ocs, and i really liked them. i havent been able to share ocs with someone in so long in a way where i took genuine interest in them. in that moment, i felt my seconds of warmth flicker inside of me. that platonic, friendly warmth which had been so familiar to me, its flame just as it was when it first died. and i felt myself feel guilty because i guess it's kind of like i'm only using him as rebound, but he was the first person ever since it has happened where i could feel real interest and passion in these characters. like i could finally play dollhouse with someone again. i felt so happy, i wanted to cry. and i wanted to cry so bad, it made me happy. 

another friend, ., seems to like me too. they told me about how they had an assignment in class instructing them to write a letter to some imaginary english-speaking friend, and they told me that they imagined the letter was for me. to think someone appreciating this friendship so much that they imagine me in such an important role. i feel a little happier. . and i have talked about my issues regarding my platonic struggles and they told me that what i was going through was painful and that i could try to think of it to the end. and that advice has been helping me a lot. think about it to the end, absorb as much as what happened until theres nothing left to absorb. then breathe it all out. breathe knowing that it hurts. we are both going through a painful time, and i don't want to hurt them at all, and they assured me i couldn't hurt them. i smiled. they trust me a lot. from what i can see, . is just as "ill" as i am. we share this similarity that brings a deep understanding in how our brains function, something that no one else would really understand because. well. if youve never felt the pain of this, then telling us to just "let it go" is easier said than done. when advising a sort of direct action, it's utterly useless and falls flat. no, i can't think of something else. i can't find distraction in the clouds, or the trees, nowhere. that's why i have to think it through. i have to internalize this pain.

Goof has been a delight as well. That poor man, I don't know how he still loves me. He's been so patient and gentle and understanding. He finally asked me about my paranoia and the distress I had been facing and i kinda just broke down. what pains me the most is that when he leaves suddenly or doesn't respond as soon as i had hoped, i start panicking and i start to think maybe he hates me, that maybe he doesnt care as much about me. then i start apologizing so he can come back. it's awful, it must be exhausting having to love someone who grows weak when the slightest bit of attention is taken away from them. but he's listened, he has listened to me and that's the craziest part. he's done research on what i'm struggling with and he's taken all these steps to make sure i feel secure and safe and in my mind i'm wondering how crazy he has to be to do all this for some idiot that's as mentally stable as a boulder on a needle. it must take a lot of patience. i don't know, it just makes me feel a bit bad since he's my friend too. i mean, you can't love someone without becoming friends, that's my philosophy. where romance fails is when there is a lack of both platonic and romantic love living side by side. 

despite all of that, i still ask if i'm missing something in my life. clearly, the universe wants me to be happy. despite that, i still feel a bit rotten. i mean yeah ive gotten over all the emotional turmoil, just barely, but i feel cold. empty. my emotions are lightweight and they float around and bump into everything, i ask myself what i did wrong. and they say it isnt my fault. that im doing fine. im okay. that im a cool person. but it doesnt feel like it. was that validation all for nothing? was i being hurt somehow? does a lack of intention mean it wasnt real? i want answers. these are answers i'll probably never get. not unless i step up and ask again for the umpteenth time. it isn't like i'll get a response anyway. mixed signals are as bad as getting no signals.
