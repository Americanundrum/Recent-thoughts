This hurts me more than it will ever hurt anyone else. I've cried, I've cut, I've begged, and nothing works. This may not be my final resort, but it's one of them. 

This used to be all I have ever wanted. This friendship, that is. It was all I ever wanted and I realized that it has been hurting me. I have tried to navigate it, tried to reconcile, tried to be patient, tried to make myself better, but nothing is enough. Nothing is good enough. And I realized this as I was speaking to a friend about it. I was speaking with so much anger and so much jealousy and agony that I think at that point I was like 'they'll never care about me the same way i care about them' and i gave up. i'll never be good enough. 

so i sat there and went through my little list of people i've been agonizing over and just removed them. i feel nothing. i feel utterly nothing. all of my emotions have been exhausted, the sobbing and the slicing, the bleeding, no more. we will be strangers again. we will all be strangers and i wont talk to them unless i have any valid reason to. i would like to say that ive forgotten them, but a large part of them still lives on within me.

because ive made art dedicated to them theyll never see. ever. ive made a bookmark for them, and it's sitting inside a book about the US electoral system. The shell of my earbud case has a design that I made because it reminded me of them. They'll never see it. i have made. so many fucking things. dedicated to a friend. who fucking thinks of me as no one. I am a stranger. and i dont know who to give them to. i hate that the version i know of them is long outdated.

to you who has read through these pages, you know how much this hurts. it hurts so much. especially when i would dream of conversations we would never have. but this is my life you know. it's just how it is. i fight to make changes to make my relationships better and this is how it is. 

this past season has been horrible for me, mental and physical issues here and there, fits of despair, my skin, my head, my everything. if we ever became friends again, i would start anew. i dont want to cry over this anymore. i dont want to think about all the times ive been hurt. and this time, im going to be honest, im going to be better, im going to be tougher. i'll be a better friend, but i will never be enough.
