Hiroshi, he's kind of like me. I function on a higher scale than him. I don't have any special abilities, but I feel lost. I feel unreliable. I feel like I'm searching for an answer that doesn't make any sense. I think the people around me are starting to tell how deluded I've become. I can't tell what's real and what isn't, I'm trapped behind memories that don't exist and lies that I want desperately to be true. I'm losing sight of myself. Every day, I perform these tasks to get ahead in life but it's as if I'm not the one doing it. When I do these things, it's like someone else takes over. someone else that is designated to keep me alive and keep my relationships stable. But when I'm alone, all of that comes down and I'm a stranger once again. I talk to people that are myself and within my mind, I think of words that I say but don't leave my mouth, only so that I can believe it's being spoken to me. I speak to Kokutai every night. As the days go on, I'm starting to think they're not imaginary, that Kutai is real. Kutai is real because they talk to me and in a sense, keep my stable. Maybe Kutai is the one controlling me when I need to live. They let me sit back and watch my life play out in front of me while i sit in the back of my mind, rotting and festering like a corpse.

I don't know who I am anymore or what I'm doing or what motivates me. Like Hiroshi Kano, I find my mind disorganized and lost, and that I can't find a key to my own emotions. i wish I could articulate it more. cant even draw. i eat on a schedule. kutai is the only reason i can function like a normal human being. kutai is the reason i can breathe. i dont feel like fjord anymore. am i more kutai than fjord? i dont know who i am. i dont know anything. i lose sight of myself.

losing these people has been more traumatizing that i thought it would be. each night i find the man behind my drawer peeking his way out a little more. maybe not visually. but when i close my eyes, and imagine the expanse of that empty space, i can see him. i can feel him. 

when you come back telling me that youre sorry, i'll look at you like a stranger. when you come back saying that you didnt mean to, i'll forgive you. i'll forgive you and i'll forget, but you'll never make me laugh like i used to. every time we talk, youll never find that fjord. you will have lost fjord by then. there will only be kutai. maybe in some way, youve killed me. youve killed me and you hid the body, but there will always be kutai to console you, even if that is me. 

i hope you never find anyone like me ever again, and i hope youre sorry.
