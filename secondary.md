I talked to my therapist earlier today. I told her that I had been reading up on certain histories and pretending that my friends are the one telling it to me. They answer my questions and tell me about how such history impacted their characters. I laugh knowing that in this world I can appreciate their wisdom. I pretend that we still talk to each other, that we're okay and that we're still friends. I think about how they're still spending time with me to tell me about their day. I make characters that remind me of them and i give them happy endings. I look for pieces of them in everyone I meet. 

She said that this was.. "poetic". That my suffering was poetic. I agree with her, and I appreciated it. Maybe my suffering is poetic, but it's so dirty and disgusting. Could you look at a thing like me, watch my body crumble while i wait for people to text me back, and tell me that I am pretty? Am I pretty for being this pathetic? I make gifts for people who will never see them, for people who would never look back to consider me. 

When I was at my timber practice today, it felt nice. it felt nice to hurt my arms while i exhausted myself. this exercise gives me a sense of life, a means of distraction. i want to be distracted these days. is my suffering still poetic when i cried every night after my roommate was asleep waiting for people to come back? was it still poetic when i was incapable of leaving my room to even eat and when i would sleep at my desk because i couldn't bother to get back to my bed? i guess it is. 

i want them back. i keep pretending they are back, but they never will be. what if one day, everyone i love leaves? i would at least like them to tell me so that i wont have to rip my hair out.
