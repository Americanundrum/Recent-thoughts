if i were to have one more conversation with you, what would it be about? 

I would tell you how wonderful of an experience it was to be your friend. how happy youve made me, and how it's like you gave me a reason to look forward to building new bridges with other people. you were the one that told me i was enough, that i could be who i am, that i didnt have to be scared. i'd tell you that you're brave and strong, and a piece of that strength still lingers in my chest. i want to rip it out, but it's so warm there. it's so small compared to the pieces of everyone else now. my lover, my brother, myself, my other friends. but your fragment is in there, silently beating and i'm waiting for you to come back to me.

i'd tell you that i'm sorry i wasn't good enough. i'm sorry that you never wanted to get close to me. i'm sorry i meant so little to you. i wish i could grab you and slice a piece of you away and store it inside of me. i'm sorry i would think of doing that, because others usually just do it willingly. see how they care about me? 

You can take as long as need and you can hate me as much as you want. i'll always be waiting for you right where i am. and when i come back, i'll make sure to do everything i can to make things right. in the mean time, i'd never tell you that i made a mimic of your persona in my head. i'd never tell you that that persona is out there living its own life with different people, it has its own interests, their own hobbies, their style, and humor. they're so, so kind to me. and sure they take a while but they never take as long as you. they have your habits, and your voice, your smile. theyre my twisted, distorted image of you. i'd never tell you that. 

but jae knows. jae knows ive been talking to "you". she thinks i'm ill. i'm just coping. all i want is one more conversation with you and maybe i'd be okay, i wouldnt be doing all of this. if we had one last conversation with each other, i'd beg you to tell me what i did wrong and why you act this way. and then i'd tell you "I'll be as patient as I have to be, I'll never let you go." I'll never, ever let that sliver of my heart go.
